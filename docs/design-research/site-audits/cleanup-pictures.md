# Cleanup.pictures audit

Inspected 2026-09-07 at 390 × 844. Direct states: landing, built-in example, brushed selection, processing, completed clean result, undo and newly revealed download. Desktop was not independently captured.

## First view and model

The working tool is immediately visible. A direct H1 describes the job, a large dashed upload target says “Tap here to load your picture,” and three example images let a user learn without supplying a file. The interaction model is upload-and-result followed by direct manipulation.

The first action is obvious without reading. The upload zone and examples occupy more visual weight than marketing. Registration is not required before value. Longer use cases, pricing, testimonial and FAQ content follow below.

## Active and result states

Selecting the built-in bag example replaced the landing shell with an edge-to-edge image canvas. The active tool exposed back, compare, settings, brush size and Clean. Brushing produced an unmistakable lime mask; after Clean completed, the masked objects were removed and a high-contrast download button appeared. Undo remained visible, and success was evident from both the changed image and new completion action.

## Mobile, trust and accessibility

The mobile layout is admirably direct and touch-sized. It avoids horizontal scrolling and moves controls around the image. Several icon buttons lacked useful accessible names, however, and the neon mask/brand accent would be too stimulating at 2 a.m. Privacy was available through a footer policy link rather than a precise statement at upload. Pricing and product cross-sell are separated below the work surface but still substantial.

## Transfer

- Let the user act immediately and offer a safe “example” equivalent through understandable answer choices.
- Change the interface visibly when input is accepted.
- Reveal completion actions only when the result exists.

## Do not import

- Do not use an upload/drop-zone metaphor for personal questions.
- Do not copy the neon highlight, bold black-and-white trade dress or image-editor control arrangement.
- Do not use testimonials, pricing or cross-sells near the assessment/result.
