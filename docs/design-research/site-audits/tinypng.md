# TinyPNG audit

Inspected 2026-09-07 at 390 × 844; current public HTML content was also reviewed. The initial upload and content shell were observed. A synthetic upload attempt was interrupted before a verifiable result, so processing, progress, download, reset and error behavior are not claimed. Desktop/tablet were not independently captured.

## First view and model

The mobile header exposes logo, Login, Sign Up and a menu. A large dashed upload area immediately follows, stating file count and size limits. A conversion toggle is adjacent. The task is obvious before reading; sign-up is not required to reach the uploader.

The upload area and panda scene carry most visual weight. Product cards, customer logos, a comparison section, testimonials and FAQ content follow. The mascot makes the service memorable, but the large branded scene is visually louder than appropriate for a 2 a.m. health-adjacent utility.

## Privacy and content

The current FAQ states that uploaded images are retained for a maximum of 48 hours, but that disclosure is far below the upload action. This is materially different from local-only processing and illustrates why data handling must be disclosed at the decision moment. The public page has useful explanatory material but also strong Web Pro, CDN, API and plugin promotion.

The accessibility tree named the upload control, format toggle, product sections, FAQ controls and footer links. Visual art does not replace the upload label. No result-state accessibility conclusion is possible from this pass.

## Transfer

- Make the first action large, bounded and self-explanatory.
- State constraints before input and place privacy beside the action.
- Keep completion visually unmistakable once verified by product tests.

## Do not import

- Do not copy the panda, illustrated environment, blue/green brand palette or playful voice.
- Do not place login/sign-up, product cards, testimonials or paid upgrades near the assessment.
- Do not bury a materially important data-retention fact in an FAQ.
