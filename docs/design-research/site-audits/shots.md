# Shots audit

Inspected 2026-09-07 at 390 × 844 and 1440 × 1000. Direct evidence: arrival/onboarding and the public editor structure visible behind it. Start Creating, media import and export completion were not exercised.

## First view and model

The first view is a dark, image-led onboarding layer: logo, “Create Amazing Mockups,” one sentence and, on desktop, one gradient Start Creating button. The underlying product is a canvas with toolbar, inspector, layout/background presets, undo/redo, Start Over and export. The largest area is the mockup preview; input and settings orbit the output.

On the 390 px capture the headline and explanation dominated and the CTA was below the visible viewport. On desktop the CTA was obvious, centered and the only primary action. Users must accept a short orientation before acting; no registration was visible at this gate.

## Working-state observations

The exposed editor structure shows media drop/paste, a central canvas, separate Mockup/Frame modes, style/border/shadow/detail controls, background choices, performance mode and export. Basic output remains central while advanced options live in side/bottom control groups. Undo, redo and Start Over are explicit. Success, error and actual export confirmation were not verified.

## Trust, accessibility and content

Privacy Policy and Terms links sit beside the Start action, but no concise data-handling promise was observed. Several icon-only buttons lacked meaningful names in the accessibility tree. Marketing sections and templates coexist in the same document, while an onboarding layer prevents that long content from displacing the editor visually.

## Transfer

- Adopt the principle that the useful output is the largest, most stable object.
- Adopt progressive disclosure and a clear restart path.
- Translate the canvas into the “One Change Tonight” card; do not reproduce its layout, colors, controls or effects.

## Do not import

- Do not gate the first question behind an onboarding CTA, especially when it can fall below a mobile viewport.
- Do not import dense style presets, cinematic effects, gradient spectacle, glass treatments or unlabeled icon controls.
- Do not make the health-adjacent result share space with dozens of customization decisions.
