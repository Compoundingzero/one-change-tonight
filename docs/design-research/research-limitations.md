# Research limitations

Research date: 2026-09-07.

## What was directly inspected

Live public pages were inspected through a real browser at 390 × 844 and, for most editor-style references, 1440 × 1000. Direct states included: Shots onboarding/underlying editor structure; IT-Tools directory and temperature converter; Cleanup.pictures initial, built-in sample, painted selection and completed result; Ray default working editor; Squoosh initial, built-in sample, encoded result and download state; Transform.tools directory and JSON-to-TypeScript output; readme.so landing and editor; Excalidraw empty canvas; current Unscreen redirect and Canva landing; Carbon default editor; and TinyPNG initial upload page.

## What was not fully inspected

- The 768 × 1024 tablet and 320 × 700 fragile-mobile passes were not completed for every external site before the browser session was stopped. Matrix cells say “not independently captured” rather than inferring success.
- No accounts were created, no subscriptions were purchased, and no login-only, paywalled or collaboration state was entered.
- Shots’ onboarding was observed, but Start Creating was not completed; its export/result details come only from the exposed public editor structure, not a completed export.
- TinyPNG’s synthetic upload attempt was interrupted before a verifiable result. The audit therefore does not claim its processing, progress or result state was observed.
- Canva’s current uploader was not used. Its active editor/result flow and Pro entitlement behavior were not verified.
- Excalidraw drawing, collaboration and export completion were not exercised.
- Carbon and Ray default samples were observed, but export completion was not triggered.
- External keyboard behavior was sampled through exposed labels and focusable structure, not a complete tab-order audit.
- No external reference received automated axe or Lighthouse testing; no conformance or performance score is claimed.

## Unscreen evidence boundary

`https://unscreen.com` was directly observed redirecting to Canva’s Video Background Remover page. The former standalone product was not treated as live. Historic notes rely only on clearly labeled public historical material: the 2020 Product Hunt listing, a 2020 remove.bg/Kaleido announcement, and the formerly indexed Unscreen API documentation. These materials establish an upload-first, automatic background-removal workflow; they do not justify invented details about every visual or error state.

## Screenshots and intellectual property

No third-party screenshots were committed. Browser captures were used transiently for research only. No reference source code, assets, logos, mascots, palette, typography combination or trade dress was copied into the product.
