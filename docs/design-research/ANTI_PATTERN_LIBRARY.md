# Anti-pattern library

## Interaction anti-patterns

- **Pre-task gate:** Shots’ onboarding and readme.so’s Get Started add a step before value. The homepage should begin the assessment directly.
- **Auto-advance quiz behavior:** it removes the chance to reconsider and feels like a funnel. Keep explicit Continue and Back.
- **False precision:** a score, probability or fixed step count would imply medical certainty. Use “about” progress and a named environmental pattern.
- **Infinite canvas:** Excalidraw’s freedom is valuable for drawing and harmful for a tired user seeking one safe action.
- **Permanent dual pane:** Transform’s input/output layout explains code conversion but becomes cramped and diagnostic-looking here.
- **Upload metaphor:** large drop zones fit files, not sensitive personal observations.
- **Tabs for essential meaning:** readme.so’s Preview/Raw split is unsuitable for safety, experiment or limitations.

## Visual anti-patterns

- Dense inspectors, toolbars and export menus from Shots, Ray and Carbon.
- Developer-dark/code-editor aesthetics from Ray, Carbon, Transform and readme.so.
- Bright playful blobs, neon masks, cute mascots or illustrated worlds from Squoosh, Cleanup and TinyPNG.
- Generic menopause pink, wellness purple, hospital blue, glassmorphism, neon gradients, flame/thermometer icons and stock portraits.
- Filling desktop whitespace with secondary cards or making every paragraph a panel.
- Tiny all-caps labels without sufficient size/spacing at zoom.

## Trust and commercial anti-patterns

- Login, upgrade, donation, testimonial, product or newsletter prompts near the first action.
- Canva-style ecosystem navigation and repeated upload CTAs.
- TinyPNG-style retention disclosure separated from the data-input moment.
- Manufacturer claims presented as independent evidence.
- “Buy now,” affiliate tracking, sponsored ranking, fake testing or an expensive recommendation before a reversible experiment.
- Sharing as a default completion action for a private health-adjacent result.

## Content and search anti-patterns

- A long hero between a search query and its answer.
- Transform-style route multiplication when pages share the same answer.
- Thin app shells that omit purpose, limitations, privacy and evidence.
- Important explanations rendered only in client JavaScript, canvas, images or SVG text.
- Hidden FAQs or fabricated medical/AI structured data.
- A content directory inside the assessment.

## Safety anti-patterns

- Diagnostic labels, hormonal-cause claims, treatment promises or improvised red-flag scoring.
- Partner-blaming language and “thermostat war” framing.
- Caveats repeated so often that they become visual noise, or hidden so far down that they cannot shape interpretation.
- An uncertainty choice that secretly maps to a confident pattern.
