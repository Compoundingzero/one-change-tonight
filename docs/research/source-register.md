# Research source register

Research date: 2026-09-07 (America/Los_Angeles)

This register supports the search-intent, SERP-gap, tool-landscape, design, SEO/GEO, and internal-linking documents in this repository. It distinguishes authoritative claim sources from representative search-result examples. Access dates are not publication dates.

## Method and limits

- Query research was a qualitative, non-personalized web-search sample run on 2026-09-07. It was not a rank tracker, did not use a signed-in Google account, and did not estimate search volume.
- “Dominant result type” means the patterns visible in the returned sample, not a census of every result or a guaranteed Google position.
- Search results vary by country, device, history, language, and time. Re-sample in the launch market in an incognito mobile browser before making rank-sensitive decisions.
- Product and publisher pages below are evidence of the result landscape or of their own interface/specification claims. They are not used as independent medical evidence.
- Health wording uses government agencies, professional medical organizations, and peer-reviewed literature first.
