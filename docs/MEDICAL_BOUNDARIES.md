# Medical boundaries

One Change Tonight organizes sleep-environment observations and low-risk comfort experiments. It cannot determine whether a medical condition, menopause, medication, infection, anxiety, or another cause is present. A cool room does not rule out a health issue; a sudden wave does not prove one.

The app does not diagnose, calculate health risk, recommend or discourage medicines/supplements, alter clinician-directed care, or claim that cooling prevents or treats night sweats. It uses one general visible boundary: new, worsening, frequent, severe, soaking, concerning, or sleep-disrupting symptoms deserve discussion with a qualified healthcare professional; seek urgent help when seriously unwell. More specific personal triage is intentionally absent.

All newly authored decision-relevant medical or emergency wording remains editorial material until an identified qualified reviewer approves it. The public product truthfully says it has not been independently medically reviewed or clinically validated.
