# Product specification

## User job

Help an adult who repeatedly wakes hot choose one small, safe, reversible environmental change by tonight, while preserving the boundary between comfort observations and medical causes. A co-sleeper may need a different thermal setup. A clinician remains the destination for new, worsening, frequent, severe, soaking, or concerning symptoms.

## Core experience

The homepage begins the first question in the initial viewport. The assessment uses at most seven primary screens plus one conditional noise follow-up, always offers “I’m not sure,” never auto-advances, supports Back, and moves focus to each legend. It returns exactly one of four primary observation patterns, relevant non-diagnostic modifiers, one experiment, visible uncertainty, “Do Not Buy Yet,” partner guidance when applicable, premium-mechanism fit without a purchase instruction, and a care reminder when rules call for one.

The result leads into a three-night browser-local tracker, print plan, print appointment note, start-over, complete deletion, and low-brightness view. `/awake-and-hot/` provides a shorter three-question action for a disrupted night and is noindex.

## Public corpus

Twenty-four query-led articles plus framework, pattern, comparison, evidence, methodology, source, editorial, boundary, privacy, terms, corrections, about, contact, and sitemap pages. Each search page opens with a direct answer and uncertainty, supplies a discriminating table and one experiment, includes a query-specific safety note and “Do Not Buy Yet” guidance, and links to both a broader concept and the next likely question.

## Non-goals

No diagnosis, clinical triage algorithm, medical treatment advice, medication or supplement guidance, account, free-text health history, affiliate commerce, brand ranking, product price feed, user profile, answer URL, runtime AI, external assessment API, or promise of traffic/adoption.
